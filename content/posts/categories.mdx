This blog has two categories, News and Guides. You set them in `site.config.ts`, under `sections.blog.categories`.

## Add a category

Add an entry with a `slug`, a `name` for its tab, and a `title` and `description` for its page. Then set that slug as the `category` of a post.

A category page exists only when at least one post is in it, so an empty category never shows a blank page.
